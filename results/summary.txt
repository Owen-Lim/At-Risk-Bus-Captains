LIMITATIONS
- Duty hours are estimated from alarm timestamps, not real driving hours.
- The risk score ranks alarm behaviour; it is not a calibrated accident probability.
- One month of alarms and a small number of accident drivers: wide intervals.
- 'Association' uses accidents from any date, so it is NOT a forecast.
- Several features are tested at once; unadjusted intervals can look significant by chance (see the multiple-test columns in association_by_feature.csv).
- Do not use these results alone for employment or disciplinary decisions.
